### How strict misses are classified

The tolerances above are applied as written in the transpile spec and are not loosened. A case that misses its tolerance is classed as *roundoff-explained* only if the C++-vs-MATLAB difference is at most 10 x the case's **roundoff floor**, a measurement of MATLAB's own rounding error for that case:

- `solveLeg`: the C++-vs-MATLAB difference must be at most max(10 x MATLAB's own error against the same solve in 60-digit `vpa` arithmetic, cond(R_PP_eq) * eps). The second term is the textbook first-order forward-error bound of a backward-stable (Cholesky/LU) solve, without its dimension factor; it covers cases where MATLAB's own rounding happens to land unusually close to the exact answer. The `Cost` output alone may also use the rounding bound of its quadratic form, nv * eps * (|d|'|R||d|) / |Cost| (Phase 4: a near-optimal cost can be ~1e10 times smaller than its terms on fuzzed legs).
- `PolyQP`, `AbortGuidance`, missions: the larger of (a) MATLAB's abs error on its final QP solve (every leg rebuilt from `QPTraj.Fix` with the `Plan.FixCg` free pattern at `QPTraj.T`, solved in `vpa`) and (b) the largest change in MATLAB's outputs when the waypoint positions and velocities are multiplied by `1 + 2*eps*randn` (3 draws, separate RNG stream; draws that change `OuterIterations` or the sample count are discarded). `AbortGuidance` uses the floor of the identical `PolyQP` call.
- `allocateTimes`: (b) applied to `FixLeg` and the initial times. `PolyEval`: (b) applied to `QPTraj.Coef` only (query times exactly at knots must not move).

Input perturbation alone underestimates the floor of the linear solves: it perturbs `R_PP` only along structured directions, while Cholesky's backward error is unstructured, so its forward error is set by cond(R_PP_eq). The `vpa` table below shows MATLAB and the C++ are equally far from the exact solution. Any other miss counts as a failure and makes the script exit with an error.

The C++ runs at the flight node capacity (`Abort::MAX_POINTS`); a C++ `CapacityExceeded` passes only if MATLAB's plan needs more nodes than that, and is then compared with MATLAB's closed-form fallback on the C++ grid (F34).

- `solveLeg` inputs from runs are rebuilt exactly as `solveAllLegs` pass 1 builds `FixP`, at both the initial and the final segment times. For the abort, passes 2-3 do not change `FixP` (F04).
- `resolveWaypoints`/`PolyQP` error IDs: 1 waypoint, r = 2, r = 7, zero first HDGRef, inconsistent HDGRef, zero MaxVel, negative MaxAccel, scaled-but-consistent HDGRef (no error).
